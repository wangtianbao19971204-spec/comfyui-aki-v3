## Default Outfit And Outfit Variants

`Morning Star default idol outfit` is the normal starting phrase for Morning
Star production. It refers to the approved wine-red and ivory bare-shoulder
idol dress, translucent gauze shoulder shawl, small flat star voice-core clasp,
very sheer ivory stockings, and wine-red high heels.

Alternate idol outfits are allowed only when a prompt, round, or caption branch
explicitly says `Morning Star alternate idol outfit`. Do not let normal scene,
pose, view, or style expansion mutate the default idol outfit into an
untracked costume variant.

When alternate Morning Star idol outfits are explored, the form identity still
must remain readable without relying on the outfit: peach-gold low twin tails,
wine-red ribbons, small gold star hairpiece, soft champagne-peach virtual eyes,
tiny star catchlight, online virtual singer demeanor, and clean orange-gold
soundwave projection language stay as the Morning Star layer.

## Allowed Variation

Vary only:

- pose and hand gesture;
- front, three-quarter, side, profile, rear, and rear-three-quarter view;
- face, bust, knee-up, and full-body framing;
- gentle expression range;
- golden virtual stage, magic-network projection, plain gold reference space,
  and clean livestream-avatar backgrounds;
- lighting intensity and rendering style, provided the form remains readable.

## Hard Rejects For Morning Star

Reject or quarantine candidates that:

- replace low twin tails / side-bundles with a single low side ponytail;
- remove the translucent gauze shoulder shawl;
- remove the star hairpiece from all visible angles where it should be seen;
- turn the outfit into a simple pure-red dress;
- turn the shawl into opaque sleeves, green sleeve covers, thick cape, long
  train, or large back cloth;
- enlarge the star clasp into a protruding chest gem or pendant;
- make the stockings opaque or replace the natural gold embroidery with dense
  mechanical stripes;
- remove the wine-red high heels or replace them with boots;
- add microphones, physical instruments, text, logo, model-sheet layout,
  extra people, or NSFW framing.
