# Ordinary Vs Morning Star Contrast Notes - 2026-08-18

User direction: keep the established Morning Star design, but push the offline/online split harder. At first glance the ordinary form and the online avatar should feel like very different images. On close analysis they should still resolve as the same adult woman.

## Shared Identity Thread

- Adult 23-year-old human woman, petite 160 cm, slender but not child-coded.
- Same soft oval face, warm sincere expression, fair warm luminous skin, and gentle approachable gaze.
- Same warm amber/gold/coral eye family and warm blond/peach hair family, but not the same pupil design, eye color emphasis, or hairstyle.
- Same memory point through a restrained red/coral/gold ribbon or star accent.

## Ordinary Alicia

- Offline daily form, not a muted idol outfit.
- Natural honey-hazel brown amber eyes with simple normal round pupils; no star-ring, star aperture, soundwave ring, or glowing virtual eye pattern.
- Loose light-brown/champagne everyday hair, grounded and natural, with a half-up tie or side-braid accent.
- Covered ivory blouse or light cardigan, simple wine-red A-line skirt, teal short shawl or small collar cape.
- Practical brown low ankle boots or similar daily footwear.
- No sheer stockings, no high heels, no off-shoulder idol bodice, no holographic stage effects.

## Morning Star

- Online magical-network singer avatar and virtual idol form.
- Soft champagne-gold to peach-gold virtual eyes with a gentle small star catchlight or subtle tiny star aperture. At most one faint soundwave crescent highlight; avoid harsh crimson, target-like rings, hypnotic spirals, and aggressive multi-ring pupils.
- Peach-gold or rose-gold low twin-tail / side-bundle hair, ribbon-bound and controlled near the body.
- Off-shoulder opaque idol bodice, compact pleated skirt, elegant high-heeled stage shoes.
- Decorative ivory chiffon overlay may be translucent, but the main bodice and skirt stay opaque.
- Stockings should be very sheer ivory: leg skin tone visible through the fabric, with natural coral-gold soundwave embroidery rather than pasted metallic stripes.

## Base Gate

Reject a base candidate if the two forms look like simple palette swaps. Also reject ordinary Alicia if she borrows Morning Star's virtual pupils, rose-gold low twin tails, sheer stockings, high heels, or off-shoulder idol bodice.
